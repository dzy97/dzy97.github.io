About Me
------
Hi! I am Zheye Deng (邓哲也). I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/) advised by [Prof. Yangqiu Song](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~yqsong/). I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/), where I was advised by [Prof. Ming Zhang](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/info/1238/2063.htm).

I have worked on structured knowledge and reasoning, from constructing large-scale knowledge graphs to extracting and integrating structural information from text. My current research interests lie in LLM post-training and agentic reinforcement learning, where I develop learning agents that combine structured reasoning and tool use to tackle complex decision-making and problem-solving tasks.
